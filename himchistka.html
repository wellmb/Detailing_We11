Interior Detailing

Interior Detailing: Factory-Fresh Leather and Lasting Odor Removal

We restore the tactile feel of Nappa leather and the velvet texture of Alcantara without leaving a glossy plastic look on the seats. Odors are handled at the source: spills, tobacco burns, mold, and moisture under the carpet each get dedicated protocols instead of a week-long fragrance mask.

Guide price from $420

About the Service

Leather needs pH balance: aggressive gas-station cleaners dry the fiber and start cracks on steering wheels and armrests. We choose chemistry for the leather type, including coated, semi-aniline, and Nappa, observe contact time, and apply conditioner after wet stages so the leather stays soft and refined matte, without sticky shine.

Odor is often not in the air but in foam, carpet, or sealants. Neutralization only makes sense after contamination is extracted and dried; otherwise you are paying for perfume that fades faster than the coffee stain in the carpet.

Alcantara and textiles receive delicate steam cleaning and test-based extraction. Where water cannot be introduced, we use steam and targeted chemistry; where depth is needed, extractor moisture is controlled so sound insulation is not soaked.

Work Stages

Five stages, from dry preparation to a finished interior with a neutral cabin climate.

  1. Dry Preparation and Air Blowout

    Vacuuming, large debris removal, and compressed air through seams and vents so dirt is not pushed into fibers once moisture is introduced.

  2. Material-Specific Chemistry

    Leather, headliner, plastic, and textile each get separate products and contact times; stitching is treated with soft brushes without overworking the coating.

  3. Steam, Extraction, and Odor Work

    Delicate steam cleaning where hygiene matters without overwetting; extractor work where liquid must be pulled from foam. If a source is found, we start the neutralization protocol.

  4. Leather and Alcantara Conditioning

    Conditioning without lacquer-like gloss, leveling of steering wheel and side bolsters, and velvet restoration without weighing down the pile.

  5. Textile Protection, Ventilation, and Handover

    Fabric protection sprays, a light OEM-style finish, and recommendations for the first day after deep moisture work.
