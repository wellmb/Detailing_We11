Ceramic Coating

Ceramic Coatings: Strong Hydrophobics and Rich Color for Years

Ceramic coating creates an ultra-smooth layer with strong hydrophobic behavior: water beads and rolls off the body, reducing mineral spotting and drying time. On properly prepared paint, ceramic coating visually concentrates the pigment, making color richer, metallics deeper, and dark shades more even in sunlight.

Guide price from $710

About the Service

Hydrophobic performance is not just marketing; it is measurable droplet behavior. With a quality top coat, the contact area on the paint is smaller and water movement is more predictable. That makes safe washing easier and reduces the risk of stubborn contamination on open parking lots.

Color saturation does not come from paint on top; it comes from optics. A leveled surface reflects light better, and the transparent ceramic layer makes the paint look cleaner. New vehicles often need only light calibration; driven cars usually need full polishing first, otherwise old defects remain under the coating.

Ceramic coating does not replace PPF for chip protection; impact loads need polyurethane. On wrapped vehicles, however, ceramic coating complements protection well, using dedicated formulas for PPF instead of body-paint protocols.

Work Stages

Five stages: paint preparation, clean chemistry, and disciplined application, because hydrophobic performance depends on the process.

  1. Defects, Layer Package, and Hydrophobic Forecast

    Inspection, agreement on base layer and one or two top coats, plus options for glass and wheels. For PPF, we use compatible formulas only.

  2. Wash and Decontamination

    Tar, iron, and wax are removed so the surface is perfectly clean for the polymer; otherwise bonding and color depth suffer.

  3. Gloss Correction / Calibration

    We remove scratches and haze because ceramic coating will only emphasize what the eye already sees. This is the base for hydrophobics and color saturation.

  4. Panel Wipe, Base, and Top Coat

    Panel-by-panel degreasing, then application with flash-time and evenness control. Depending on the package, a second top coat adds stronger hydrophobic behavior and gloss.

  5. Curing and Handover

    Protocol-based curing, first-wash instructions, and parking recommendations during the sensitive period so water behavior and color depth remain predictable.
