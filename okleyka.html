PPF Wrap: Chip Protection and Self-Healing Micro-Scratch Defense

Protective polyurethane absorbs impacts from sand, gravel, and wash brushes, keeping the factory paint intact. Modern PPF top coats are self-healing: with gentle heat from sunlight or warm water, fine scratches and light webbing in the film level out without repainting or polishing the body.

Guide price from $2,150

About the Service

PPF is a thick transparent shield: it absorbs the edge impact of stones and helps prevent chips from reaching the primer. Unlike wax or spray protection, film stays on the body for years without changing the design; with precise installation, edges and curves stay discreet even on dark paint.

Self-healing works within the limits set by the film manufacturer: surface scratches and light sponge marks clear from the top layer when care rules are followed. Deep cuts and punctures still require local film replacement, so we account for those scenarios while mapping the wrap.

At Apex Detail, we do not hide defects under film: anything visible through transparent polyurethane is corrected during paint preparation. You get protection from new chips and a showroom-level finish without compromise.

Work Stages

Five stages, from selecting coverage zones to handover with clear care and self-healing guidance.

  1. Paint Inspection and Wrap Map

    Inspection under dedicated lighting, with paint thickness readings when needed. We agree on the package: impact zones, front-end coverage, full-body coverage, headlights, and inner sills, then lock it into the estimate before work begins.

  2. Wash and Chemical Preparation

    Safe removal of tar, iron deposits, and organic contamination. The surface must be sterile before it meets the adhesive layer of the PPF, otherwise clarity and installation durability suffer.

  3. Paint Preparation for Polyurethane

    Polishing or local correction when needed: scratches and swirls are not hidden under the armor. The goal is a clean base that still looks right through the film years later.

  4. Cutting, Heat Forming, and Installation

    Film panels are fitted to the body, heated under control around curves, and cleared of installation fluid. We check seams, sensors, and emblems so the film is not under stress at risky corners.

  5. Finish, Acceptance, and Self-Healing Guidance

    Remaining fluid is removed and the vehicle is reviewed with you. We explain how to activate self-healing after fine scratches and what to do during the first 48-72 hours after installation.
