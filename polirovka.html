Paint Correction

Multi-Step Paint Correction and Mirror Gloss Without Compromise

Professional polishing removes scratches, wash swirls, and oxidized clear coat while controlling paint thickness. The result is even mirror gloss: the body reflects its surroundings in one clean line, without sandy haze or holograms from rushed previous polishing.

Guide price from $580

About the Service

Scratches and wear cannot be polished away with wax; they must be corrected in sequence with abrasives, reducing pad aggression and refining the compound finish. We choose the cut for your paint, whether factory, repainted, or a thin repair layer, and rely on thickness readings in key areas.

Mirror gloss is not a lucky highlight on the hood; it is a consistent reflection curve across the body. After correction, metallic and ceramic colors look deep again, and dark finishes stop breaking into cloudy patches in daylight.

Before ceramic coating or PPF, the surface is always degreased with a panel wipe, so protection lays evenly, keeps its hydrophobic behavior, and preserves the gloss we just achieved.

Work Stages

Five stages, from documenting defects to a clean surface ready for ceramic coating or PPF installation.

  1. Inspection and Measurements

    Dedicated lighting, documentation of scratches, swirls, and oxidation, plus thickness readings on the hood, fenders, and doors. We agree on correction depth: one-step, two-step, or multi-step.

  2. Wash and Decontamination

    Removal of iron deposits, tar, and resin. Polishing only starts on a fully reset surface, otherwise dirt particles can be driven into the clear coat.

  3. Mechanical Preparation

    Clay or modern alternatives, seam and molding checks, and degreasing before abrasive work begins.

  4. Multi-Step Correction and Mirror Finishing

    Defects are removed step by step while pad aggression is reduced, followed by jeweling and ultra-finishing. We remove polishing holograms and create a wet, clear reflection.

  5. Anti-Static, Panel Wipe, and Protection Prep

    Oils and silicones are removed from the paint, followed by final inspection. The body is ready for ceramic coating or PPF installation without losing mirror clarity on control panels.
