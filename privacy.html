Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Detail collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information for visitors to this website and clients of our detailing studios in Los Angeles and Miami.

1. General Provisions

Apex Detail ("Apex Detail," "we," "us," or "our") operates premium automotive detailing studios in Los Angeles, California, and Miami, Florida. This Policy applies to personal information we collect through this website, by phone, email, messaging, and in person at our studios. By using the website or submitting a service request, you acknowledge this Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Name and contact details, including phone number and email address, that you provide in booking forms, calls, emails, or messages;
  • Vehicle information, such as make, model, condition notes, service preferences, and license plate number when needed to provide services or maintain service records;
  • Website and device information, including cookies, IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referral information, and visit time, as needed for website functionality, security, and analytics.

3. Purposes of Processing

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, prepare estimates, provide detailing services, manage client relationships, process payments where applicable, improve our studios and website, maintain security, send service-related communications, and comply with legal, tax, accounting, and insurance obligations.

4. Legal Bases and Retention

We process personal information based on your consent, our need to perform or prepare a service agreement, our legitimate business interests, and compliance with applicable U.S. federal, state, and local laws. We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including warranty, accounting, dispute resolution, insurance, and legal recordkeeping needs, then delete, de-identify, or archive it according to our retention practices.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, hosting, CRM, appointment scheduling, analytics, payment processing, communications, insurance, accounting, legal support, and similar business functions. These providers are expected to use information only for the services they provide to us. We may also disclose information if required by law, subpoena, court order, government request, or to protect rights, safety, property, or prevent fraud.

6. Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, support core site functions, measure traffic, understand how visitors use the site, and improve user experience. You can limit or block cookies in your browser settings, but some website features may not function properly. By continuing to use the website after the cookie notice appears, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this Policy.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about certain personal information we maintain, and to opt out of certain uses where applicable. We do not discriminate against individuals for exercising privacy rights. To submit a request, use the contact details listed on the Contacts page or email ApexDetail@apexdetail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, legal obligations, or business practices. The current version is posted on this page. Last updated: May 3, 2026.
